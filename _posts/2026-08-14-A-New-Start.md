Sure looks a lot different here! Figured it would be best to actually move towards a Github Pages site in order to post and blog rather than running Wix. Looks unprofessional to have it through a .wixsite, and I'm sure as heck not paying those guys the sheer amount they ask for when I could just host a site here. Plus it's more fun to make things myself anyway. Jekyll will be a bit of a learning curve but I'm determined to land on a nice theme and organize my stuff well. The start is always the hardest, right?

Speaking of, this is sparked by a feeling of necessity to voice what I'm doing. I feel as though I'm pretty competent as a developer, game dev, artist, what-have-you. However, I don't think this comes through in my old site or through my resume. I'd rather this site be a true example of all the different interests I have and the projects I work on. I'm always on a journey to get better and make new things, and it needs to be surfaced in some way. I'm not really one to brag, and the thought of having to keep writing out posts about what I'm doing is initially tiresome and daunting. But the fact is, if I'm going to be heading towards the greater heights I really want to, then it's gonna go a long way to get used to this stuff.

So here's to a new start, a new page, and a whole lotta blog posts ahead of me.
